How billing works

Unified billing

One Raptor account per organization. Subscription billing and prepaid credit balance flow through a single Stripe Customer record. One bill, one audit domain, one set of access controls.

Workspace billing cycle

  1. Subscription billed at start of monthly cycle (base + seat add-ons only)
  2. Usage tracked throughout cycle against tier inclusion
  3. At tier inclusion limit: service pauses by default
  4. To continue: opt in to prepaid credits at your tier-discounted rate
  5. Credits decrement per governed response; no metered overage on subscription invoice

Credit balance

  • Purchase credits at any time ($10 minimum)
  • Each governed response decrements balance at your applicable rate
  • Inference pass-through decrements at provider cost + 10%
  • Low-balance notification at 20% of last purchase
  • At zero balance: service pauses or continues based on your API key configuration
  • Auto-top-up available (threshold, amount, monthly ceiling — all customer-configured)
  • Credits do not expire
  • Credits are non-refundable

Pause vs. continue

At API key creation, choose the key's behavior when credit balance reaches zero:

  • Pause at zero (default for production keys): governed responses pause until credits are added
  • Capped auto-continuation (default for non-production keys): auto-top-up within your monthly ceiling

This choice is front-loaded at setup, not discovered at incident time. Change it anytime per key.
